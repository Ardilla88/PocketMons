# Game Mechanics

## Spawn Rates

### Wild Encounters (Grass)
When moving through grass, there is a **15% chance** per step to trigger a wild encounter.

The encounter table is as follows:

*   **Fernorlax**: 5% chance
*   **Other Monsters** (RedMon, BlueMon, GreenMon): 95% chance (distributed evenly among remaining types)

### Fishing
When fishing (facing water):
*   **Hook Chance**: 30%
*   **Pokemon**: Always **BlueMon**
